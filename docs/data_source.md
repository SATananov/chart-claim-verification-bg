# Data source

The file `eu_unemployment_2019_2025.csv` contains a small fixed excerpt
of the annual unemployment rate for the European Union.

- Unit: percentage of the labour force
- Age group: 15 to 74 years
- Period: 2019 to 2025
- Source: Eurostat
- Dataset: `une_rt_a`

The values were prepared from the annual Eurostat article published
with data extracted in May 2026. The article reports the 2025 value and
the yearly percentage-point changes from 2020 to 2025.

Sources:

- https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=Unemployment_statistics_and_beyond
- https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/une_rt_a/default/line?lang=en
